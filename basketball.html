DraftMatrix: Fantasy Hoops Support

DraftMatrix: Fantasy Hoops is a fantasy basketball draft companion for iPad and iPhone: a live, tier-based draft board with a draft clock, pick tracking, keepers, targets, multiple drafts, custom rankings import, and on-device AI pick suggestions.

Common questions

Where do the rankings come from?
Draft ranks and average draft position are compiled from publicly published fantasy basketball draft data. Tiers are computed on your device from that blended consensus. You can also import your own rankings as a CSV.

Categories or Points?
Both. Switch scoring in the Draft panel under Rankings (on iPad it's in the toolbar) and the whole board reloads and re-tiers — Categories uses roto-oriented ranks, Points uses points-oriented ranks.

Do AI suggestions send my data anywhere?
No. Suggestions run entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence. Nothing about your draft leaves your iPad or iPhone. (Requires a device with Apple Intelligence.)

Where is my draft saved?
On your device, and in your personal iCloud so a draft can move between your devices. We never see it.

The clock says it's my pick but it isn't.
The clock counts every pick you mark in the app, yours or not. Set your league size and slot in the Draft panel and mark each pick as it happens.
