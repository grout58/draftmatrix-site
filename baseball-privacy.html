DraftMatrix: Fantasy Baseball Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your draft never leaves your device and your own iCloud. The app sends a few anonymous usage counts, described below.

What the app stores, and where

Your draft data — picks, rosters, keepers, targets, imported rankings, league settings, and preferences — is stored on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, the same data is stored in your personal iCloud key-value storage so a draft can move between your devices. This data belongs to you, is protected by your Apple ID, and is never transmitted to Grout Technology LLC or anyone else.

AI features

Draft suggestions are generated entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence (Apple's on-device foundation models). Your roster and the player pool are processed locally and never leave your device.

Network requests

The app downloads publicly available fantasy baseball data: draft ranks, average draft position, player positions and team codes, from a public content API. These requests fetch public data and include no personal information or identifiers about you. Standard server logs on those third-party services (such as IP addresses inherent to any internet request) are governed by their respective policies.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends a small number of anonymous signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, so we can see whether the free version is working for people. A signal says only that one of these things happened: the app was opened, a mock draft was started or finished, a free pick was used (and how many are left), the DraftMatrix Pro screen was shown, Pro was bought or restored, or a draft-day reminder was set.

Each signal also carries general information about the device and app: the app version, the iOS version, the device model and screen size, language, region and time-zone settings, accessibility settings such as text size, and how many times and on how many days the app has been used.

Signals never contain player names, your picks, your roster, league or team names, imported rankings, your draft date, or anything you type.

Signals from the same device are grouped by an anonymous identifier that is scrambled (hashed) on your device before it is sent. It is not your Apple ID and not an advertising identifier, it is not linked to your name or email address, and it is not used to follow you across other apps or websites.

Draft-day reminders

If you set a draft date, the app schedules its reminders on your device. The date stays on your device.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or profiles
  • No crash-reporting SDKs, and no analytics beyond the anonymous signals described above
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of data. Your draft is never sent to us, and the anonymous signals go to TelemetryDeck and nowhere else

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous signals described above contain nothing that identifies a person.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
