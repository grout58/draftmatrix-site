DraftMatrix Support

DraftMatrix is a fantasy football draft companion for iPad and iPhone: a live, tier-based draft board with pick tracking, keepers, targets, multiple drafts, custom rankings import, and on-device AI pick suggestions.

Getting help

The in-app Guide (the ? button in the toolbar) explains every control. For anything else, contact us and we'll get back to you:

  • GitHub: github.com/grout58

Common questions

Where do the rankings come from? Tier rankings are fetched from publicly published data at borischen.co; bye weeks and ADP come from fantasyfootballcalculator.com. You can also import your own rankings as a CSV.

Do AI suggestions send my data anywhere? No. Suggestions run entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence. Nothing about your draft leaves your iPad or iPhone.

Where is my draft saved? On your device, and in your personal iCloud so a draft can move between your devices. We never see it.
