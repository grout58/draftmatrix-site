Features

Built for in-person drafts

Online draft rooms give you a live board, a pick clock and best available at a glance. Draft around a table and you're usually back to paper. DraftMatrix brings that online drafting experience to your offline draft: mark each pick as it's called, and the board, the tiers, the clock and your roster keep up.

  • Your scoringPPR, Half PPR or Standard. Switch formats and the rankings reload for that format.
  • Tiers that warn youTier counters turn orange when a tier runs low and red on its last player.
  • Track every pickOne tap crosses off a player another team takes; star the players you take. Undo any mistap.
  • A pick clockRound, overall pick and how many picks until your turn, snake or linear.
  • Live ActivityThe draft clock on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Keepers and targetsKeepers are marked drafted without moving the clock. Bookmark sleepers and filter the board to them.
  • Find anyone fastFilter by position, search every list, or hide drafted players.
  • Private pick suggestionsSuggest Pick uses Apple Intelligence on your device to weigh your roster against who's left and explain its pick. Nothing leaves your device. Requires iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.
  • Siri and ShortcutsAsk for a suggested pick or your team by voice.
  • Set up in a minuteA setup guide on first launch: scoring, number of teams, your draft slot and roster.
  • More than one leagueMultiple drafts with independent boards, synced between your devices through your own iCloud.
  • Works offlineCached rankings and a built-in snapshot keep the board going without a connection. Import your own rankings from a CSV, and share your finished roster.

FAQ

Questions

What's free?
The download, the board, tiers, search, filters and the draft guide, marking other teams' picks, and your first three picks to your own team (three in total, across all your drafts).
What does Pro add?
Unlimited picks to your own team in every draft. It's a one-time in-app purchase, $4.99 in the US (local prices vary), with no subscription. Pro for football is bought in this app; the other sports' apps unlock separately.
Where do the rankings come from?
Tier rankings, bye weeks and average draft position come from publicly published fantasy football data. You can also import your own rankings as a CSV. See Support for details.
Does it collect my data?
No. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your draft stays on your device and in your own iCloud. Read the privacy policy.
What do I need?
An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. Suggest Pick needs iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.
