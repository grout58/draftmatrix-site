FAQ

Questions

Where is the Draft panel?

Most settings below live in the Draft panel. On iPhone, tap the gear button at the top left of the board. On iPad, it's the column on the left.

How do I restore DraftMatrix Pro?

DraftMatrix Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, with no subscription. To get it back on a new device or after reinstalling, sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with, open the Draft panel and tap Restore Purchases in its last section. It's also under the buy button on the Pro screen.

Once Pro is active, that section reads “Pro — unlimited drafts” or “Pro — included with your purchase”. Pro is bought separately in each app: restoring in the hockey app doesn't unlock the football app. Restoring needs a connection to the App Store.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

The download is free, and so are the board, tiers, search, filters and the Guide, marking other teams' picks, keepers and targets. Your first three picks to your own team are free too (three in total, across all your drafts). Pro unlocks unlimited picks to your own team in every draft.

How do I turn the Lock Screen draft clock on or off?

The draft clock also runs as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and, on iPhones that have one, in the Dynamic Island. It starts when you mark the first pick of a draft, updates with every pick you mark, and ends when your roster is full, when you reset the draft or when you switch drafts.

There's no switch for it inside the app. To turn it off or back on, open the Settings app, tap the DraftMatrix app's name and use its Live Activities switch.

The clock says it's my pick, but it isn't.

The clock counts every pick you mark in the app, yours or not. In the Draft panel, set Teams, My draft slot and Snake draft to match your league, and mark each pick as it happens. Undo takes back a mistap.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The board opens with a built-in snapshot of the rankings, then refreshes when it can. Without a connection it keeps the last rankings it downloaded, or the built-in snapshot if it has none. Your picks are saved on your device as you go.

The Rankings section of the Draft panel shows which you're seeing (for example “Live”, “Cached” or “Built-in snapshot”) and when it last updated. Tap Refresh now when you're back online.

How do I add keepers?

Long-press a player, choose Keeper…, then My keeper or Another team's keeper. Keepers are marked drafted, with a “K” badge, but don't move the pick clock. To undo one, long-press the player and choose Clear keeper.

Does my draft sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, through your own iCloud, when both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account. Your drafts, picks, keepers, targets and roster setup sync on their own; there's nothing to turn on. Imported CSV rankings stay on the device you imported them on.

How do pick suggestions work, and what do I need?

Tap Suggest in the Best available section of the Draft panel (⌘I with a keyboard on iPad). Apple Intelligence weighs your roster against who's left and explains its pick; tap Draft to my team to take it, or Again for another. Drafting a suggested player counts as one of your picks.

Suggestions run entirely on your device, and nothing about your draft leaves it. They need iOS 26 and a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. If the app says the model is still downloading, try again in a few minutes.

Can I import my own rankings?

Yes. In the Draft panel's Rankings section, tap Import CSV rankings… and choose a CSV or plain-text file with the columns Rank, Name, Tier, Position. With a header row the columns can be in any order, and a Team column is optional. Leave out Tier and the app computes tiers for you. Files can be up to 2 MB and 2,000 players.

Your picks, keepers and targets carry over. Switch Use imported rankings off to see the regular board again, or tap Remove import.

Where do the rankings come from?
  • Football: publicly published expert tier rankings for each scoring format, plus public average draft position and bye-week data.
  • Hoops: publicly published fantasy draft ranks and average draft position, category ranks for Categories leagues and points ranks for Points leagues. Tiers are computed on your device.
  • Hockey: publicly published expert rankings blended with public average draft position, with positions from public team rosters. Tiers are computed on your device.

Rankings refresh when the app opens, and every 10 minutes while Auto-refresh every 10 min is on (in the Rankings section). To change scoring, use Scoring in the Rankings section (football: Standard, Half PPR or PPR; hoops: Categories or Points).

Hockey: why does a player show as “F”?

Some published rankings list forwards without a specific position. The app refines them to C, LW or RW from roster data; a player who can't be matched stays “F” and can fill any forward slot.

How do I start over, or run more than one draft?

Tap Reset in the Draft panel and choose Reset picks (keep keepers & targets) or Reset everything. Rankings stay either way, and Undo can bring the board back. For another league, tap the Draft panel's title and choose New Draft…; each draft keeps its own board.

Does DraftMatrix collect my data?

No. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and the apps collect no data. Your draft stays on your device and in your own iCloud. The privacy policies: football, hoops, hockey, baseball. The optional email list and contact form on this website are covered in each policy's website section.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. Pick suggestions need iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.
