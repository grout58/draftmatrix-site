In person

Built for in-person drafts

Online draft rooms give you a live board, a pick clock and best available at a glance. Draft around a table and you're usually back to paper. DraftMatrix brings that online drafting experience to your offline draft.

  • Works offlineCached rankings and a built-in snapshot keep the board going without a connection.
  • Every pick, every teamTap a player when another team takes them; star the ones you take. Undo any mistap.
  • A pick clockRound, overall pick and picks until your turn, for snake or linear drafts.
  • On your Lock ScreenA Live Activity puts the draft clock on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Keeps the screen awakeSo the board is still there when it's your turn.
  • Your devices, in syncYour draft syncs between your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud.

Pricing

What's free, and what Pro adds

DraftMatrix Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It's $4.99 in the US; local prices vary. Pro is unlocked separately in each sport's app.

Free

  • The download
  • The board, tiers, search, filters and draft guide
  • Marking other teams' picks
  • Your first three picks to your own team (three in total, across all your drafts)

DraftMatrix Pro

  • Unlimited picks to your own team, in every draft
  • Paid once. No subscription
  • Restore Purchases is in the Draft panel

No ads, no account, no tracking. The App Store privacy label for each app on the App Store reads “Data Not Collected.”
