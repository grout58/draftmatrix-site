Features

Built for in-person drafts

  • Roto or PointsSwitch between Roto (5x5 categories) and Points scoring, and the board reloads and re-tiers to match your league.
  • Tiers per positionC, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS, OF, DH, SP and RP, with tiers computed on your device from published draft ranks blended with average draft position.
  • Tier warningsTier counters warn when a tier runs low and flag the last player in it.
  • Best available, roster needsThe top remaining player at every position, and what your lineup still needs.
  • Track every pickCross off other teams' picks, star yours and undo any mistap. Keepers are marked drafted without moving the clock.
  • Targets and filtersBookmark targets before the draft, then filter by position or to your targets, search, or hide drafted players.
  • A draft clockRound, overall pick and picks until your turn, snake or linear, with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Private pick suggestionsSuggest Pick uses Apple Intelligence on your device and explains its pick. Requires iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.
  • Multiple drafts, in syncRun more than one draft with independent boards, synced between your devices through your own iCloud.
  • Your own rankingsImport rankings from a CSV file, or export the board.
  • Built for in-person draftsCached rankings and a built-in snapshot keep the board going offline, and the screen stays awake.
  • Same pricing modelA free download with a one-time DraftMatrix Pro purchase, no subscription. Pro for baseball is bought in this app.

Questions

Does it work on iPhone Duo?
Yes. It's built for iPhone Duo: unfold it for your board and your draft side by side; fold it mid-draft and pick up right where you left off.
What do I need?
An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. Suggest Pick needs iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.
