What it's planned to be

These are in the current pre-release build. Details may change before launch.

  • Roto or PointsSwitch scoring and the board reloads and re-tiers to match your league.
  • Tiers per positionC, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS, OF, DH, SP and RP, with tiers computed on your device from published draft ranks blended with average draft position.
  • Best available, roster needsThe top remaining player at every position, and what your lineup still needs.
  • Track every pickCross off other teams' picks, star yours, undo any mistap. Keepers and targets too.
  • A draft clockRound, overall pick and picks until your turn, snake or linear, with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen.
  • Private pick suggestionsSuggest Pick runs on your device with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence required).
  • Built for in-person draftsCached rankings and a built-in snapshot keep the board going offline, and the screen stays awake.
  • Same pricing modelPlanned as a free download with the same DraftMatrix Pro one-time purchase as the other apps.
