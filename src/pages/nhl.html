Built for in-person drafts

Online draft rooms give you a live board, a pick clock and best available at a glance. Draft around a table and you're usually back to paper. DraftMatrix brings that online drafting experience to your offline draft: mark each pick as it's called, and the board, the tiers, the clock and your roster keep up.

  • Tiers per positionC, LW, RW, D and G, with tiers computed on your device from a blend of published expert rankings and average draft position.
  • Tiers that warn youTier counters warn when a tier runs low and flag the last player.
  • Best available, roster needsThe top remaining player at every position, and what your lineup still needs.
  • Track every pickOne tap crosses off a player another team takes; star the players you take. Undo any mistap.
  • A draft clockRound, overall pick and how many picks until your turn, snake or linear. Also as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Keepers and targetsKeepers are marked drafted without moving the clock. Bookmark targets and filter the board to them.
  • Private pick suggestionsSuggest Pick uses Apple Intelligence on your device to weigh your roster, your open starter slots and who's left, and explain its pick. Nothing leaves your device. Requires iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.
  • Siri and ShortcutsAsk for a suggested pick or your team by voice.
  • Set up in a minuteA setup guide on first launch: number of teams, your draft slot and roster.
  • More than one leagueMultiple drafts with independent boards, synced between your devices through your own iCloud.
  • Works offlineCached rankings and a built-in snapshot keep the board going. Import your own rankings from a CSV, or export the board.
  • Draft-night detailsKeeps the screen awake, dark mode, and keyboard shortcuts on iPad.

Questions

What's free?
The download, the board, tiers, search, filters and the draft guide, marking other teams' picks, and your first three picks to your own team (three in total, across all your drafts).
What does Pro add?
Unlimited picks to your own team in every draft. It's a one-time in-app purchase, $4.99 in the US (local prices vary), with no subscription. Pro for hockey is bought in this app; the other sports' apps unlock separately.
I paid for the app before it was free.
Pro is yours at no charge. If it doesn't show, use Restore Purchases in the Draft panel.
Why does a player show as “F”?
Some published rankings list forwards without a specific position. The app refines them to C, LW or RW from roster data; a player who can't be matched stays “F” and can fill any forward slot.
Where do the rankings come from?
They're compiled from publicly published expert fantasy hockey rankings and public average-draft-position data, with positions from public roster data; tiers are computed on your device. You can also import your own rankings as a CSV. See Support.
Does it collect my data?
No. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your draft stays on your device and in your own iCloud. Read the privacy policy.
What do I need?
An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. Suggest Pick needs iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.
