NHL DraftMatrix Support

NHL DraftMatrix is a fantasy hockey draft companion for iPad and iPhone: a live, tier-based draft board with a draft clock, pick tracking, keepers, targets, multiple drafts, custom rankings import, and on-device AI pick suggestions.

Getting help

Common questions

Where do the rankings come from?
Player rankings are read from NHL.com's publicly published fantasy hockey ranking articles; positions are confirmed against public NHL roster data; average draft position comes from ESPN's public fantasy hockey player list. Tiers are computed on your device from that blended consensus. You can also import your own rankings as a CSV.

Why does a player show as “F”?
NHL.com lists forwards without a specific position. The app refines them to C, LW or RW from roster data; a player who can't be matched stays “F” and can fill any forward slot.

Do AI suggestions send my data anywhere?
No. Suggestions run entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence. Nothing about your draft leaves your iPad or iPhone. (Requires iOS 26 and a device with Apple Intelligence.)

Where is my draft saved?
On your device, and in your personal iCloud so a draft can move between your devices. We never see it.

The clock says it's my pick but it isn't.
The clock counts every pick you mark in the app, yours or not. Set your league size and slot in the Draft panel and mark each pick as it happens.
